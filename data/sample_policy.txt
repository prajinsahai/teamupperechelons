COMMERCIAL PROPERTY AND BUSINESS INTERRUPTION POLICY. Limit of Liability: USD 5,000,000 per occurrence and USD 10,000,000 in the aggregate.
Deductible: USD 250,000 each and every loss. Business interruption is subject to a waiting period of 48 hours before cover attaches.
Exclusions: This policy does not cover loss arising from war, terrorism, nuclear risks, or cyber events including data loss.
Contingent business interruption from suppliers is excluded unless endorsed. Sub-limit: USD 1,000,000 for flood.
Conditions: The insured shall notify within 30 days of any occurrence. Territory: worldwide excluding sanctioned countries.
